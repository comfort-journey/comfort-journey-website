## DESTINATIONS GRID

### 6 India Escapes

**1. Coorg: The Coffee Coma**
Wake up to mist. Drink coffee that was grown 10 feet away. Read a book. Do absolutely nothing. The air is so clean it almost feels illegal. You'll forget what traffic sounds like.
*Vibe Check:* Cozy, misty, quiet.
*Perfect For:* When you want to escape human interaction.
*Starts at ₹15K. Worth every penny.*

**2. Udaipur: Royal Treatment**
Because sometimes you just want to feel rich. Palaces, lakes, and rooftop dinners that make you forget your EMI. Drink wine while looking at a lit-up palace. It's the ultimate main character energy.
*Vibe Check:* Bougie, aesthetic, romantic.
*Perfect For:* Anniversaries, or just treating yourself because you survived the week.
*Starts at ₹18K. Live a little.*

**3. Goa (The Quiet Side): No EDM, Just Waves**
South Goa. Empty beaches. Good seafood. A cold beer. The Goa you actually need right now. No crowded shacks. No loud music. Just the sound of the ocean and the breeze in the palm trees.
*Vibe Check:* Susegad, slow, salty.
*Perfect For:* When you need to stare at the horizon and rethink your life choices.
*Starts at ₹20K. Pure peace.*

**4. Rishikesh: River & Reset**
Not the crowded parts. The quiet cafes by the Ganges. Maybe some yoga. Definitely some peace. Eat a wood-fired pizza while watching the river flow. It's the perfect mix of chill and spiritual.
*Vibe Check:* Spiritual but make it modern.
*Perfect For:* Finding your zen without giving up good coffee.
*Starts at ₹12K. Find your center.*

**5. Lonavala/Khandala: The Classic Monsoon Fix**
Drive up. Eat Maggi. Look at the valley. It's a cliché because it works. Sometimes you just need the classics. The fog, the rain, the hot chai. It hits different every single time.
*Vibe Check:* Nostalgic, breezy, easy.
*Perfect For:* Impromptu road trips with the squad.
*Starts at ₹10K. Quick and easy.*

**6. Pondicherry: French Toast & Bicycles**
Yellow walls. Croissants. Walking around aimlessly. It's like Europe but you don't need a visa. Rent a scooty and explore the cafes. The vibe is unmatched.
*Vibe Check:* Artsy, slow-paced, colorful.
*Perfect For:* Pretending you're in a French indie movie.
*Starts at ₹16K. Très chic.*

### 6 International Quickies

**1. Dubai: The 48-Hour Flex**
Fly out Friday night. Shop, eat, party, desert safari. Back by Monday morning. It's fast, it's loud, it's exactly what you need to shake things up. You'll sleep when you're dead.
*Vibe Check:* High energy, luxury, non-stop.
*Perfect For:* When you want to feel like a VIP.
*Starts at ₹45K. Go big.*

**2. Colombo: The Island Hop**
Short flight. Amazing crabs. Colonial vibes. It's right there and it's perfect. Walk by the ocean, eat spicy food, drink good tea. It's a totally different country but feels like home.
*Vibe Check:* Tropical, relaxed, flavorful.
*Perfect For:* A quick international stamp without the jet lag.
*Starts at ₹35K. Spicy and sweet.*

**3. Bangkok: Street Food & Foot Massages**
Eat Pad Thai on the street. Get a massage that fixes your posture. Repeat. The energy is unmatched. The food is unbeatable. It's chaotic in the best way possible.
*Vibe Check:* Chaotic good, delicious, vibrant.
*Perfect For:* Foodies and shopaholics.
*Starts at ₹40K. Unbeatable.*

**4. Kathmandu: Momos & Mountains**
Cool air. Temples. The best momos of your life. A totally different world, just 2 hours away. Walk around Thamel and soak in the vibes. It's raw, real, and refreshing.
*Vibe Check:* Chill, cultural, crisp.
*Perfect For:* A complete change of scenery.
*Starts at ₹30K. High altitude chill.*

**5. Maldives: The "I Need To Disconnect" Trip**
Just water. And you. And maybe a cocktail. The ultimate flex. Wake up, jump in the ocean, eat, sleep. No shoes required. No stress allowed.
*Vibe Check:* Ultimate luxury, isolated, blue.
*Perfect For:* When you literally want to be on an island away from everyone.
*Starts at ₹65K. Treat yourself.*

**6. Singapore: Clean, Green, and Foodie Heaven**
Changi airport is a vibe itself. Eat at hawker centres. Walk around Gardens by the Bay. It's so efficient it'll make you cry. Everything just works.
*Vibe Check:* Modern, organized, delicious.
*Perfect For:* A hassle-free, futuristic weekend.
*Starts at ₹55K. Effortless.*
