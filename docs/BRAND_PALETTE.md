# 🎨 Comfort Journey — Official Brand Color Palette & Design System

> **Established 1992** · *"YOUR JOURNEY • YOUR COMFORT • We cover Distance with Comfort"*

---

## 🌟 The 5 Core Brand Colors

| Color Name | HEX Code | RGB | Role & Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vivid Tangerine** | `#FF892F` | `rgb(255, 137, 47)` | **Primary Brand Action & Energy**: Booking CTAs, Price highlights, Active search buttons, Star ratings, glowing highlights. |
| **Beige / Warm Cream** | `#F9FBE7` | `rgb(249, 251, 231)` | **Primary Light Shade**: Replaces harsh pure `#FFFFFF` across headings, body typography, modal backgrounds, and warm editorial contrast. |
| **Electric Aqua** | `#6FE6FC` | `rgb(111, 230, 252)` | **Secondary Sky & Ocean Accent**: Tropical packages, live weather ticker, AI Trip Planner highlights, interactive links, borders. |
| **Lime Cream** | `#DAF561` | `rgb(218, 245, 97)` | **Tertiary Energy Accent**: Verified traveler checkmarks, trust badges, discount ribbons, adventure vibes. |
| **Deep Navy** | `#001D51` | `rgb(0, 29, 81)` | **Primary Dark Base**: Replaces flat black (`#000000`) for the background, glass panels, navbars, and card surfaces. |

---

## 🌓 Dark & Light Harmony Rules

1. **Backgrounds**: Use **Deep Navy** (`#001233`, `#001D51`, `#052669`) with subtle radial gradients of `#FF892F` and `#6FE6FC` instead of stark pure black.
2. **Typography**: Use **Beige Cream** (`#F9FBE7`, `#EDF3D2`) for headings and text instead of stark pure white.
3. **Badges & Accents**: 
   - Experience / Luxury: **Vivid Tangerine** (`#FF892F`)
   - Oceans / Weather / AI: **Electric Aqua** (`#6FE6FC`)
   - Trust & Verified: **Lime Cream** (`#DAF561`)
